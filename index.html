VENUS ♀

Most organisations deploying AI systems know they need to think about safety and compliance. They just do not know where to start. Frameworks like the EU AI Act and the NIST AI RMF are comprehensive, but reading them does not tell you what to actually do with your specific system in your specific context. VENUS is our attempt to make that first step less daunting. You describe your system or your question, and the tool works out which safety dimensions matter for your situation, which tools are worth looking at, and where your compliance gaps are, producing a report you can actually hand to someone.
